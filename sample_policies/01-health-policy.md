# Meridian Health Plus

## About this sample
This is a fictional demonstration policy for testing PolicyLens. It is not issued by an insurer and creates no insurance contract. All amounts are illustrative Indian rupees (INR). This sample describes an individual health plan with a sum insured of INR 500,000 per policy year.

## Hospitalization coverage
Medically necessary inpatient hospitalization lasting at least 24 hours is covered up to the sum insured, subject to the policy exclusions and waiting periods. Listed day-care procedures are covered even when hospitalization is less than 24 hours. Room rent is limited to INR 5,000 per day; ICU room rent is limited to INR 10,000 per day. Expenses above the room rent limit are payable by the insured.

## Waiting periods
The initial waiting period is 30 days from the policy start date, except for hospitalization due to an accident. Pre-existing conditions have a waiting period of 36 continuous months from the first policy start date. Cataract, hernia, and joint replacement have a specific waiting period of 24 months, unless medically required because of an accident. Completing a waiting period does not remove other exclusions or limits.

## Exclusions
Cosmetic surgery is excluded unless medically necessary following an accident or covered cancer treatment. Routine dental treatment, non-prescription supplements, infertility treatment, and experimental therapies are excluded. Maternity expenses are excluded under this sample health plan. Hospitalization solely for diagnostic evaluation is excluded.

## Pre and post hospitalization
Eligible medical expenses incurred during the 30 days before admission and 60 days after discharge are covered when directly related to an accepted inpatient claim. Bills and prescriptions are required. The expenses count toward the annual sum insured.

## Cashless and reimbursement claims
For a planned cashless admission, request pre-authorization at a network hospital at least 48 hours before admission. For an emergency admission, notify the claims desk within 24 hours of admission. Cashless approval is subject to verification of eligibility and policy terms. For reimbursement, submit a completed claim form, discharge summary, itemized hospital bills, payment receipts, and prescriptions within 30 days after discharge.

## Co-payment and deductible
There is a 10% co-payment on every admissible health claim for an insured person aged 60 or above at the policy start date. There is no co-payment for an insured person under age 60 in this sample plan. There is no annual deductible. The insured pays non-admissible charges in addition to any applicable co-payment.

## Renewal
The policy period is one year. The renewal grace period is 30 days after the premium due date. Coverage is not available for incidents occurring during a gap between expiry and receipt of the renewal premium. Renewal does not increase the sum insured automatically.
