# Horizon Comprehensive Motor

## About this sample
This is a fictional motor insurance policy for demonstrating document retrieval. It is not an actual insurance product or offer. Illustrative amounts are Indian rupees (INR). The insured declared value of the example private car is INR 800,000.

## Own damage coverage
Accidental collision, theft, fire, flood, cyclone, and malicious damage to the insured vehicle are covered subject to deductibles, exclusions, and the insured declared value. Flood damage to the vehicle body is covered. Engine damage caused by water ingress or starting a flooded engine is excluded unless the engine-protection add-on is listed in the policy schedule. Wear and tear, depreciation, and mechanical or electrical breakdown are excluded.

## Third party liability
Third-party bodily injury and property damage are covered subject to the limits shown in the policy schedule. This sample does not specify a monetary third-party liability limit. The policy schedule must be consulted for the applicable limit. Personal belongings left inside the vehicle are not covered.

## Deductibles
The compulsory deductible is INR 1,000 per admissible own-damage claim for an engine capacity up to 1,500 cc and INR 2,000 for an engine capacity above 1,500 cc. A voluntary deductible, if selected in the policy schedule, applies in addition. The sample vehicle has an engine capacity of 1,200 cc and no voluntary deductible.

## Exclusions
Driving without a valid driving license, driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs, using a private vehicle for commercial hire, and damage outside the geographical area stated in the schedule are excluded. Consequential loss and normal wear and tear are excluded. A zero-depreciation add-on does not waive compulsory deductibles.

## Motor claim procedure
Notify the insurer promptly after an accident and before repairs begin. Provide photographs of the damage, the policy number, the registration certificate, the driver's license, and a completed claim form. Obtain a police report for theft, third-party injury, or when required by the insurer. Do not authorize non-emergency repairs before the surveyor's assessment. For theft, provide both original keys when available and the police report.

## Roadside assistance
The roadside assistance add-on includes towing up to 50 kilometers, battery jump-start, and flat-tire assistance. Fuel delivery is available, but the cost of the fuel is payable by the insured. Assistance is limited to four events per policy year. Roadside assistance does not pay for replacement parts or repair labor at a garage.

## No claim discount
A no-claim discount may apply at renewal after a claim-free year, according to the renewal schedule. This sample does not specify the discount percentage. An own-damage claim may affect this discount; a roadside assistance request alone does not affect it under this sample.
