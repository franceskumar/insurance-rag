# Evergreen Term Life

## About this sample
This is a fictional term life insurance policy used only to demonstrate PolicyLens. It is not a real product or a contract. All amounts are illustrative Indian rupees (INR). The example sum assured is INR 10,000,000 and the policy term is 20 years.

## Death benefit
If the life insured dies during the policy term while the policy is in force, the insurer pays the sum assured to the nominated beneficiary, subject to the policy exclusions and claim verification. The example death benefit is INR 10,000,000. There is no maturity benefit or cash value if the life insured survives the term. Accidental death does not automatically double the death benefit.

## Eligibility and disclosure
The sample entry age range is 18 to 60 years. Medical underwriting may be required. The applicant must accurately disclose medical history, occupation, tobacco use, and other information requested in the application. Eligibility is subject to acceptance by the insurer. This sample does not guarantee acceptance or specify a premium.

## Suicide exclusion
For death due to suicide within 12 months from the risk commencement date or the latest policy revival date, the death benefit is not payable. Instead, 80% of total premiums paid, excluding taxes and rider premiums, is payable to the beneficiary, subject to policy terms. This sample does not define any other suicide-related payment.

## Premium grace period and lapse
The premium grace period is 30 days for annual, half-yearly, or quarterly payments and 15 days for monthly payments. Coverage continues during the grace period. If the premium remains unpaid after the grace period, the policy lapses. Revival requires insurer approval and may require new health declarations and payment of overdue premiums.

## Death claim documents
The beneficiary should notify the claims team and submit a completed death claim form, the death certificate, the original policy document or acceptable alternative, beneficiary identity and bank details, and relevant medical records. Accidental or unnatural death may require a police report and post-mortem report. The claims team may request additional documents to verify the claim.

## Riders
Critical illness and accidental death riders apply only if explicitly selected in the policy schedule and the additional rider premium is paid. Rider definitions, waiting periods, and exclusions are separate from the base policy. This sample includes no active riders.

## Nomination
The policyholder can request a beneficiary nomination update by submitting the nomination form with the required identity details. A nomination update takes effect when registered by the insurer. This sample does not address inheritance law or disputes among beneficiaries.
